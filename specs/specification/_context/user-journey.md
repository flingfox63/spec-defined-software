# Specification authoring journey

A requirement author records the actor, initial business state, goal, boundaries,
and expected outcome in durable context. Each acceptance criterion must follow
from this scenario, including negative cases. When multiple interpretations
would change an outcome, the author compares them against the scenario and
records the accepted reason; unresolved product choices require user input.

Review notes can disappear after delivery. Accepted requirements must remain
understandable and verifiable without depending on temporary review documents.
Business readers own the what and why; technical design explains how each
accepted criterion will be implemented and independently verified.
